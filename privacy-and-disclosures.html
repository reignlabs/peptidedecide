Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This standalone policy describes how [Legal Entity Name] ("we," "us"), operator of PeptideDecide.com, treats "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act (MHMDA) and similar laws. It contains only the information those laws require.

1. What consumer health data we collect

When you use our questionnaire, you may voluntarily provide health-related information, including: age, sex assigned at birth, height, weight and approximate BMI; health conditions, diagnoses, and goals you select; medications or medication categories; and any free text you choose to enter.

How our tool currently handles this: Your questionnaire answers are processed in your browser to generate your on-screen briefing and are not transmitted to or stored by us unless you take an additional step — generating or requesting your PDF, or providing your email. 2. Sources

We collect this data directly from you, through the on-site questionnaire and any forms you submit.

3. How we use it

  • To generate your educational briefing and, if you request it, your PDF dossier.
  • To email your dossier to you, only if you opt in.
  • To produce de-identified, aggregate analytics that help us improve the service.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. By default we do not share your identifiable health data. Where you request a service or opt in, we may share the minimum necessary with: [PDF/email service provider], our payment processor [Stripe] (which receives payment data, not your health answers), and [named lab / provider partners, if and only if you choose to use them]. A current, specific list of recipients is maintained here: [link or list].

5. Consent

We obtain your affirmative, opt-in consent before collecting consumer health data beyond what is necessary to provide a service you have requested, and we obtain a separate opt-in consent before sharing it. We do not use deceptive design to obtain consent. Any new category of data or new purpose will require updated notice and new consent.

6. Your rights

  • Confirm & access the consumer health data we hold about you, including the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with.
  • Delete your consumer health data.
  • Withdraw consent to our collection and sharing.
  • Appeal a denied request.

We will respond within 45 days (with one 45-day extension when reasonably necessary) and will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact [privacy@peptidedecide.com] or use [request form link]. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity.

7. Data security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the volume and nature of the data, meeting a reasonable standard of care for our industry, and we restrict access to those who need it to provide a service you requested.

8. Geofencing

We do not use a geofence around any in-person health care facility to identify, track, or send notifications to consumers.
